# Roadmap

This roadmap describes proposed work. Items below are **not implemented**, funded, contracted or validated unless a later release explicitly says otherwise.

## Months 1–2 — governance and co-design

- Completed for this source-only release: ownership authority confirmed and MIT/CC BY 4.0 split licences adopted.
- Complete child-safeguarding, privacy, accessibility and threat-model reviews.
- Interview teachers and learners with appropriate consent.
- Define low-bandwidth and offline acceptance criteria.
- Establish baseline usability and pedagogical observation protocols.

## Months 3–5 — reusable open activity kit

- Extract documented activity interfaces and starter templates.
- Add localisation hooks and right-to-left layout checks.
- Create openly licensed, low-bandwidth media alternatives.
- Add automated accessibility, privacy and browser tests.
- Publish teacher facilitation guidance and adaptation examples.

## Months 6–8 — proposed teacher-facing AI scaffolding

- Prototype an optional teacher authoring assistant for adapting prompts and question sets.
- Keep generated material draft-only until a teacher reviews and explicitly publishes it.
- Add provenance, uncertainty and safe-fallback interfaces.
- Prohibit learner profiling, emotion inference, automated language grading and high-stakes decisions.
- Evaluate local or low-connectivity operation and document any unavoidable data flows.

## Months 9–10 — controlled pilot preparation

- Complete data protection impact assessment and safeguarding sign-off.
- Prepare training and incident-response materials.
- Recruit pilot participants only after documented organisational consent.
- Run accessibility and security review on the pilot build.

## Months 11–12 — pilot, evaluation and release

- Conduct a limited teacher-led pilot.
- Evaluate usability, participation patterns and teacher workload without claiming causal learning impact beyond the study design.
- Publish anonymised methods and aggregate findings where consent permits.
- Release verified code, documentation and separately cleared assets under the approved licences.
