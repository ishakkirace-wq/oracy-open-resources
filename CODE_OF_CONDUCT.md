# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and child-safe community.

Participants must:

- communicate with care and assume good faith;
- welcome differences in language, disability, culture, identity and experience;
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, sexualised conduct and intimidation;
- protect private information, especially information about children and schools; and
- never use project access to contact, profile or test on children without appropriate authorisation and safeguards.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens safety, privacy or constructive collaboration. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact channel on the repository profile.
