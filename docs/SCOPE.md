# Scope and claims

## Included now

The runnable prototype contains six static, teacher-led activities:

- Sleeping Dragon;
- Magic Train;
- Listening Detectives;
- Story Machine;
- Emotion Studio; and
- Describe & Draw.

They demonstrate interaction patterns and pedagogical intent. They do not reproduce all visual media, account features or deployment integrations from the commercial teacher-resource system.

## Explicitly outside this repository

- Oracy World;
- OracyNet;
- WordPress plug-ins and production-site configuration;
- log-in, account, subscription and customer-management features;
- school, teacher or learner records;
- proprietary production media and brand assets;
- application-video production materials;
- original Sleeping Dragon animation/image assets and other uncleared production media; and
- proposed AI services.

## Claims not made

This repository does not claim:

- that the activities improve learning outcomes;
- that an institutional pilot is confirmed;
- that artificial intelligence is currently implemented;
- that a model can assess a learner's oracy, language level, emotion or behaviour;
- that relative microphone amplitude is a calibrated decibel measure; or
- that possession of a source archive establishes copyright ownership or redistribution rights.
